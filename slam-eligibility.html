Stay eligible to play

Athletics are a privilege. Students must be present, academically accountable, and in good standing to participate in practices, tryouts, games, races, and contests.

Weekly · Academics

Home Run

Every week, the homeroom teacher checks whether your athlete kept up in class — work done and turned in. Meeting it earns a green Home Run for the week.

  • Classwork completed
  • Homework turned in
  • Keeping up in every subject
Weekly · Behavior

Bulls Commitment

The same weekly check covers behavior. Athletes show the SLAM High 5 — respect, effort, and good choices. Meeting it earns a green Bulls Commitment for the week.

  • Respectful to staff and teammates
  • On task and giving best effort
  • Following school and classroom rules
How it works

Probation & Suspension

Each week the teacher marks your athlete green (met it) or red (missed it) for Home Run and Bulls Commitment. A green week keeps them in good standing. A red week starts a two-step ladder — and any green week resets it right back to the start.

  • 1st red week — Probation. Your athlete still plays, but you and the coach are notified so everyone can help them get back on track.
  • 2nd red week in a row — Suspension. Your athlete sits out the next game. Once it's served, they start fresh.
  • Any green week — back to good standing, ladder reset.
Example

How it plays out

Say a student misses homework the week of Sep 11 — they're marked red, placed on probation, and still play that weekend. The next Friday, Sep 18, they turn everything in — green — and they're back to good standing.

Later they're marked down two Fridays in a row, Oct 2 and Oct 9. The first is probation; the second is a one-game suspension, so they sit the next game. After serving it, the ladder resets and they start clean.

F
Academics

No Failing Grades

On top of the weekly check, report-card grades are reviewed at each eligibility grade check. A student with an F in a core subject across two grade checks in a row may receive a one-game suspension. If no games are left that season, it carries to their next sport and is served in the first eligible game.

Attendance

Attendance & Absences

Students who are chronically absent are ineligible for athletics — attendance matters before and during the season.

Students must attend school on the day of a practice or competition. Miss school, and they can't practice, try out, or play that day.

Consistent practice attendance is part of game eligibility. Each head coach sets the team's practice policy and works with students in other activities when possible.

Final call

Athletic Director Decision

Students represent SLAM with positive behavior, academic effort, and teamwork. Final eligibility and discipline decisions rest with the athletic director.
